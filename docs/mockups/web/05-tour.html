Questions

Fair questions.

Does anything leave my Mac?

Not to us. Recording, text from your screen, transcription and search run on your Mac. Ask AI uses the provider you choose, and you can pick a local model so even that stays home.

Will it slow my Mac down or eat my disk?

It's built to run all day in the background. You choose how long to keep things, and recording pauses itself if your disk runs low.

Can I keep some apps out?

Yes. Hide any app and it is never recorded. You can also pause everything with one click.

What happens when the trial ends?

Mnema switches to read-only. Everything you recorded stays browsable, searchable and askable. Only new recording pauses until you buy.
