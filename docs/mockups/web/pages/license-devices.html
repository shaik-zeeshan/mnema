Move Mnema to a new Mac

Your Mnema license works on up to 3 Macs. If you just tried to activate a new one and were told the limit is reached, you can free up your devices yourself — right from the app, no forms, no waiting.

  1. Open Mnema on the Mac you want to use.
  2. Go to Settings → About → License & Trial.
  3. Click Free up my devices.

That one click clears the license's device list and activates the Mac you're on. To keep the limit meaningful, it works once every 30 days — if you reset recently, the app shows the date it opens up again.

Still stuck? Email support@mnema.day and we'll sort it out.
