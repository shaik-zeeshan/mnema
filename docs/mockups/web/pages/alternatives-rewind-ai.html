The short version · 01

What happened to Rewind AI

Late 2022
Rewind launched in late 2022 as a Mac app that recorded your screen and audio locally and made it searchable — “the search engine for your life.” For a while it was the reference point for personal screen memory on the Mac.
2024
In 2024 the company rebranded to Limitless and pivoted to a cloud-based wearable, the Limitless Pendant. The Mac app stopped being the focus.
Dec 2025
In December 2025, Meta acquired Limitless. The Rewind Mac app shut down on December 19, 2025, Pendant sales stopped, and service was discontinued in the EU and UK, with remaining support winding down through 2026.
Now
If you relied on Rewind, you're now looking for a replacement — and the shutdown itself is a useful lesson in what to look for next.
Choosing a replacement · 02

What to look for in a replacement

A tool that records everything you see and hear is only as good as its answer to one question: who else can see it? Four things worth insisting on.

  • Stays on your MacCapture, text extraction, transcription, and search should all run on-device, with the record encrypted at rest. If a company can read your history, an acquisition can too.
  • Captures everything you'd want backScreen, audio, and activity — not just screenshots. The moments you need to recall are as often something said in a call as something on screen.
  • Search plus ask-AIKeyword search finds what you can name; asking an AI about your own past finds what you can only half-describe.
  • You control retention and deletionKeep history forever or auto-delete on a schedule, and delete any stretch of it in one click. Your record, your rules.

Questions · 04

Frequently asked

Is Rewind AI still available?

No. The Rewind Mac app shut down on December 19, 2025, after Meta acquired Limitless (the company Rewind became). Pendant sales stopped, service was discontinued in the EU and UK, and remaining support winds down through 2026.

What is the best Rewind AI alternative for Mac?

If you want what Rewind originally promised — a private, searchable record of everything on your Mac — Mnema is the closest match: it captures screen, audio, and activity on-device, encrypts it at rest, and lets you search it, scrub back to any moment, and ask an AI about it. Screenpipe (source-available) and OpenRecall (open source) are cross-platform options worth a look too.

Does Mnema send my recordings to the cloud?

No. Capture, text extraction, transcription, and search all run on-device — there's no account, no telemetry, and no Mnema cloud to send anything to. The one exception is opt-in: you can use your own Deepgram account for higher-accuracy transcripts, and audio leaves your Mac only if you turn that on.

Can I still use my Rewind data?

Rewind stored its recordings locally, so the files may still be on your Mac — but Mnema does not import Rewind archives. Starting with Mnema means starting a fresh record from the day you install it.
