The fine print

Questions people ask

What happens when the trial ends?

Mnema switches to read-only. Everything you recorded stays browsable, searchable, and Ask-AI-able — only new recording pauses until you buy.

What happens after my year of updates?

Nothing breaks. The app keeps working forever on the builds you own. If you want another year of new builds, renew for $29 — whenever you like, with no penalty for lapsing.

Is there a subscription?

No. $69 once. The optional $29 renewal is a purchase you make on your terms, not a plan that charges you.

How many Macs can I use it on?

Up to 3 at a time. Your license key is a signed string you paste into the app. Replaced a machine? Free up your old devices yourself from Settings, once every 30 days.
