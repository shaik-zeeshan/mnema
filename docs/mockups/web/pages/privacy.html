Privacy

What we collect, and what we never touch

Mnema records your screen and audio, so the honest answer matters more here than a page of legal boilerplate. Everything Mnema captures stays on your Mac. The only data that reaches us comes from this website, buying a license, and emailing support.

Never leaves your Mac

What Mnema never sends anywhere

  • Your screen recordings, screenshots, audio, or transcripts — these are written to an encrypted database on your Mac and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Anything about which apps you use, what you type, or what you search for inside Mnema.
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports from inside the app.

If you turn on a cloud AI provider or cloud transcription in Settings, the app sends data to your own account with that provider, using your own API key or subscription sign-in. That is your arrangement with them — it never passes through us.

Who touches what

The four services involved

Umami Cloud
Anonymous website analytics: page views, referrer, country, browser, and download-button clicks. So we know whether the site explains the product well enough for people to try it. No cookies, no fingerprinting, no personal data — nothing that identifies you as a visitor.
Polar
Your email and billing details when you buy a license. Polar is our merchant of record — it takes the payment, handles tax, and issues receipts. We never see or store your card details.
License server (mnema.day)
A one-way hash of your Mac's hardware id, plus your license key, when the app activates. So a license runs on up to 3 Macs and can't be shared endlessly. After activation the app verifies offline. The hash can't be reversed into anything about you or your machine.
Email (support)
Whatever you write to support@mnema.day, and your email address. So a human can answer you.
Your call

Removing your data

Recordings are yours to delete from inside the app at any time — we can't delete them for you, because we never had them. For anything we do hold (your purchase record, your support emails, your license activations), write to support@mnema.day and we'll remove it.

Questions about any of this go to the same address. If this page ever disagrees with what the app actually does, treat it as a bug and tell us.
