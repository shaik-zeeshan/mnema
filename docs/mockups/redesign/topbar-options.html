A · Unified toolbar

Recommended

What. One 38px strip, Safari/Xcode-style: traffic lights, a compact Timeline | Insights switch, a centered “Recall or ask…” field that doubles as the chat door (⌘↵ asks), and Ask ⌘J on the right.
Why / cost. Keeps both surfaces one click away and visible (the app only has two), drops the brand and help icon, and puts search where the eye lands. 44px → 38px, no empty middle.

B · Icon rail

What. No top nav at all. A 48px vertical rail holds Timeline, Insights, Chat, then bell + settings; the titlebar keeps only page title, search and Ask.
Why / cost. Scales if sections grow and frees the whole strip, but spends 48px of width on every page (Timeline’s rail and Insights’ sidebar both want that width) for just two destinations.

C · Contextual titlebar

What. Titlebar shows where you are (breadcrumb), a ⌘K command-palette chip in the middle, and a chat icon. Surfaces move into the palette and ⌘1 / ⌘2.
Why / cost. Quietest and most “native”, but hides the main switch behind a shortcut: a new user can’t see that Insights exists. Better as a later step once the palette is real.
