Chosen

Favorites first, with folding provider groups inside "Browse all". Most switches are between two or three models, so this puts those on one short screen with number keys, and the long lists only appear when you ask for them. The trade-off: finding a brand-new model takes one extra click ("Browse all") unless you just type.

00What's wrong today

Too much scrolling

Every provider is expanded, one after another. With OpenRouter connected that's about 170 rows in a 290 px box, and Ollama is at the very bottom.

No memory

The picker never learns what you use. Switching back to the model you used five minutes ago means searching or scrolling again.

01Favorites first

Opening the picker shows only what you switch between: the default, your favorites with number keys (press 1–9), and the last two models you used. "Browse all" opens the full list; typing searches it directly. Hover a row to make it the default. Outside the picker, ⌘⇧M jumps to the next favorite without opening anything.

  • Good: a two-model habit becomes one keystroke; the picker stays small no matter how many providers you connect.
  • Costs: number keys only work while the search box is empty (otherwise you couldn't type "4o"). New models are one click further away.
