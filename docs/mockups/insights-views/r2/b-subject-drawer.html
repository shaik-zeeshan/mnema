02How it behaves

  1. 01Open a subjectThe whole window is the subject: big title, four numbers, the full-width chart, then every conclusion grouped Strongly held → Forming → Just taking shape → Fading.
  2. 02Turn a tier's pageA tier with more than fits shows "1–5 of 9 ‹ ›" at the end of its heading. The heading never moves; only the rows below it change. ←/→ turn the section under the pointer.
  3. 03Click a conclusionThe drawer slides in on the right at full window height. The page narrows to make room, and the row you clicked stays where it was, shaded. The chart picks out its line.
  4. 04Walk the list↑/↓ move to the next or previous conclusion, crossing tiers and tier pages; the drawer follows and starts at its top.
  5. 05Page its evidence8 entries at a time, newest first, pager in the drawer's "Evidence" heading. Paging the drawer never moves the page, and the other way round.
  6. 06Close itEsc or × closes the drawer and the page widens again. In a narrow window the drawer covers the page as a full sheet with a back arrow.

03Trade-offs

Good at

The page is whole again: nothing about the subject is shrunk to a strip while you're just reading. The drawer is always the full window height, so a conclusion's statement and its evidence are never split, and the row you picked stays visible next to it. ↑/↓ make checking many conclusions quick. Familiar from Mail and Finder's preview.

Costs

With the drawer open, the page gets about 56% of the width: the title and numbers stack and the rows lose their evidence counts. Two things scroll on their own, which is one more thing to understand. In a narrow window the drawer covers the page, so you lose the row-beside-proof view there.

What changes in the app. SubjectDetail.svelte goes back to the full page (big title, four stats, full chart) and gains a drawer beside it, open only when a conclusion is picked; the slim SubjectStrip.svelte is no longer needed on wide windows. ConclusionRail.svelte becomes the full-width list grouped by tier, each tier paging 10 at a time. EvidencePane.svelte moves into the drawer unchanged apart from its pager. SubjectChart.svelte already bolds one conclusion; it just follows the open one. Pager.svelte becomes the small "11–20 of 69 ‹ ›" that sits in a heading.
