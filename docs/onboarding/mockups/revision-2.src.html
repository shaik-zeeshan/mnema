Nothing floats at the bottom of a column, and Change settings is four screens.

Two changes to chosen-cinematic-rewind.html, plus the one refresh it was overdue: every control below is the real input component, inlined live from input-components/parts/ — the same designs that shipped as CaptureSentence, ExcludedApps, FeatureSwitches, Providers, ModelPickers and AiSetup. 1 · The hole on the left. Setup, Voice and the Finale pinned their action to the bottom of a 360px column whose content ran out after two lines, so the screen showed a column-height gap with a button parked under it. Every working screen now ends in one full-width footer, and the two columns centre against each other. Nothing is pinned inside a column any more.

2 · One screen for four sections. Change settings scrolled four sections past a 150px rail — which was both the densest screen in the flow and the other place a short column left a void. It is now five screens, one section each, reached by the tab strip that replaces the rail. Four sections, not the old four: Engines and Models split (together they overran a screen), and Storage is gone — it repeated screen 03's sentence exactly, and one setting does not get asked twice. Capture rate, retention and the folder are settled on Capture & Storage, one Back away. The phase bar still counts it as step 4 of 8: it is one round trip, not four new steps.
