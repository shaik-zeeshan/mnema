10Context — what you told it, and what it concluded

The bento earns its keep here by holding two different kinds of belief side by side without ever mixing them up. Left column: statements you wrote — editable, deletable, and exempt from decay. Right column: what the engine inferred from your activity, shown only as a read-out with its confidence. The composer is a 4×1 tile whose payload is a text field rather than a row list, and the guardrail and the outlives-retention disclosure are tiles of the same object, not footnotes.

Correction — the three things you can actually change

Authored statements are yours: edit rewrites them in place, delete removes them, and both take effect immediately with the autosave chip's row echo. Inferred conclusions are the engine's: you cannot rewrite the sentence, only Pin it (exempt from decay) or Dismiss it (reset behind a 2× resurface bar) — and those two controls live on the belief itself, in Subjects. A dismissal is never a delete, which is why the Dismissed tile exists at all.

What this page promises, and what it deliberately does not

Context is the one surface where wrong copy is a privacy claim rather than a cosmetic bug. Every line below is the app's own wording, kept because paraphrasing it would change what it promises.

Claim on the pageWhy it is worded that way
Authored statements never fade from your dossier.Authored context genuinely carries no confidence and no decay — unlike a conclusion, which is recency-weighted with a 30-day half-life.
This derived understanding deliberately outlives your raw-capture Retention Policy window…ADR 0029. Retention Cleanup deletes recordings and does not cascade into user_context_*. Saying "your data is deleted after 90 days" on this page would be false.
Wipe User Context is the only control that clears it.Named, not linked to a fake button: the control lives in Settings › Intelligence › User Context, and it is destructive enough to keep in one place.
Restoring lets one form again — only if your activity still supports it.Restore lifts the veto; it does not resurrect the old row. A restored belief has to earn its way back over a 2× evidence bar.
Health, politics, sexuality, religion… are never inferred or surfaced.The guardrail's categories are engine-internal and never serialised to the UI — the page names the policy, never a per-fact "suppressed" badge that would leak the classification.
Not drawn: a "pending review" queueThere is no pending/accepted state machine. A conclusion is visible, faded, dismissed or superseded; an authored statement is live the moment you add it.
Not drawn: categories on authored rowstopic is a free-text string the user types. The eight-value category enum belongs to activities, not to context, and colouring authored rows by it would invent a taxonomy.
