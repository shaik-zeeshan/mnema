The jump control — one pill, two jobs

Click the readout and the jump panel opens under it, as a bento: three quick targets, seven day tiles carrying their own coverage bar and hours, then the month. Days with no recording are disabled — the control never lets you land on an empty day. There is no “from / to”: a history scrubber has a position, and the visible span is a consequence of the zoom segment in the bar.

Secondary controls — five buttons became two clusters

The shipping bar carried a date jumper, an engine chip, rerun, a text toggle and refresh — five peers, none of them ranked. Now: navigation on the left (step, zoom, coverage readout), evidence on the right (engine chip + hit count), and the two rare verbs move into one overflow menu where they can carry their state and their keyboard equivalents.
