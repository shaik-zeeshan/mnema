Deviations, stated

WhatWhy
Tile radius is 12, not the group idiom's 10The bento clips media at every tile edge; 10 read tight against a full-bleed frame. The inner radius is then derived (12 − 6 inset = 6) rather than picked.
Tile inset is 14, not 16One optical correction so a 349px result tile keeps a 16:9 frame at the full cell width. It is held on every tile, so it is a constant, not a one-off.
The AudioDrawer floats above the rail-wrap instead of over itThe shipping drawer covers the tick rail, so you cannot scrub while listening. Same drawer, same anatomy, anchored 132px up. Order is unchanged.
Search and Ask have no segmented controlA segmented control makes them peers competing for one field. Ask is reachable as the first result row and by ⌘⏎, and once you are in it the whole chrome changes. Mode identity without a mode switch.
Retention lives under Capture, not DataIt is the second half of the frame-rate decision; keeping the cost and the keep-window on one pane is worth the IA move. Data keeps storage location, export and access.
