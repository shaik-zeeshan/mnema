Two layers, one page

The deletion sentence — exactly as true as the backend is

Retention Cleanup and Delete Recent Capture do different things to this page's data, and the difference is the whole point of the surface. This callout sits under the standing list; it promises nothing the store does not implement.

Your context outlives your captures

When screen and audio age out under your retention window, what Mnema learned from them stays — the summaries become the evidence. Delete Recent Capture is different: it erases what was derived from that window too, and drops any conclusion left without enough evidence to stand. To erase the understanding itself, use Wipe User Context in Settings — it clears every activity, conclusion, dismissal and saved chat, and turns the engine off first.

Retention Cleanup — captures only Delete Recent Capture — captures and what was derived from them Wipe User Context — the whole dossier, engine off

Authored statements on this page survive all three of the capture-side operations; only Wipe removes them.

Honesty audit — what this page refuses to draw

No Pin, no Dismiss here

Both commands exist, but they belong to a conclusion, and conclusions are corrected on Subjects. The Steering card on this page is read-only — a chevron on it would be a dead affordance.

The steering link is an illustration

Nothing in the store records “this authored statement produced that conclusion”. The card shows your top three visible conclusions and the fact that authored context steers derivation — it does not claim a per-row causal link, and the supports word is the weakest true verb available. Wiring a real link is a backend ask, not a mockup.

“Restore” is not undelete

Dismissing deletes the conclusion row and records a veto. Restore lifts the veto; the belief only comes back if a later derivation pass still finds evidence for it. The copy says exactly that.

No confidence on an authored statement

AuthoredContext has text, topic, createdAtMs, updatedAtMs — and nothing else. No score, no status, no decay, so no bar, no percentage, and no “pin”: pinning a thing that never fades is meaningless.
