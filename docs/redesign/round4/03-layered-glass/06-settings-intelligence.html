The five custom inputs — and where each one earns it

The rule this direction holds itself to: a bespoke control only where the value has a physical consequence the stock control cannot state — gigabytes, degrees, a conflict, or what survives. Everything else is a switch, a pop-up button or a segmented control, because a settings screen full of instruments is a themepark, not an app.

1 · Capture rate Capture › Video

Frames per second, priced in the unit you actually care about — with the denominator that makes it legible.

0.52 48 fps
2 fps → ≈ 1.4 GB / day ≈ 42 GB / month · of 494 GB free
2 · Retention Data › Storage

Not a duration drop-down: a ladder, with what you already have marked on the same axis as what you are choosing to keep.

7 days30 days90 days 1 yearForever
34.2 GB kept now 126 GB projected

Frames, audio and transcripts older than 90 days are deleted nightly. Context notes and conversations are not touched — they are what Mnema learned, not what it recorded.

3 · OCR duty cycle Intelligence › OCR & Search

Two-ended by nature, so it is drawn with both halves. The consequence is written underneath in prose, not in percent.

While recording
20% cools 80%
While paused
40% cools 60%

Reading 42 frames takes about 9 minutes and holds the fans off. At 50% the backlog clears in half the time and this Mac runs roughly 8 °C warmer while it does.
