Look at: this is the settings surface complete, in this direction's floating-rail shape. Nothing here is invented: the five groups, their twenty-two sections and all 96 indexed rows are the ones the app renders today, plus the shortcut editor's four nested cards and the MCP connector list, which the index cannot see. Pages 05 and 06 showed the shell and the instruments; this page proves the structure holds when you pour the whole app into it.

The rail, corrected to the real structure

05 drew a re-grouped eleven-item rail (Setup / Intelligence / Data / About). The app has five groups and twenty-two sections, and twenty-seven rows will not fit a 208px rail at native density. So the rail lists the five groups, and the active one discloses its sections — the pane's sticky headers already carry the same names, which is exactly what the shipping app does. The floating-glass rail, the ⌘F field and the pinned cost footer are unchanged from 05. The number on a group is its indexed row count, so ⌘F and the rail agree.

Structure audit — what the index can and cannot see

Not every indexed row is a setting

Ten of the 96 are pure status or placeholder rows — Validation, Active Device, OCR availability, the four Selected model status cards, AI runtime, Derivation status. They are drawn here as read-only blocks, not controls, so the surface never implies a value you can change.

Twenty-one shortcut actions are invisible to ⌘F

The shortcut editor renders custom markup, not the indexed row primitive. Only the umbrella Global shortcuts toggle has an index entry — so “seek forward” finds nothing. Worth fixing, but the mockup states it rather than pretending otherwise.

MCP breadcrumbs as “Providers”

The connector list sets its section to intelligence, so ⌘F breadcrumbs it Intelligence › Providers even though its card is titled MCP connectors. Drawn as it is, not as it should be.

Destructive actions live inside rows

Wipe User Context, Delete unused OCR/transcription models, Delete speaker model, Delete voiceprint, Revoke grant and Delete Log File are all children of another row — never their own. Every one of them is drawn in .btn--danger, which is the only reason they are findable at a glance.
