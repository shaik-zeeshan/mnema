Direction 03 — Layered Glass

05 · Settings — the rail floats, the save state never hides

Look at: the sidebar stops being a grey column welded to the content. It is a floating translucent rail — inset, rounded, shadowed — with a search field at the top, four labelled groups, and the live cost of what you enabled pinned to its bottom (the LM Studio move). The pane beneath it is one opaque scroll surface with sticky section headers. Autosave is stated twice and clipped never: a chip in the title bar, and a “Saved” echo in the row you actually changed.

1100 × 720 — General. Shortcut rows are keycap recorders; the second one is armed and reports its conflict under the field, naming the owner. Save state: idle chip, top right.

Autosave — four states, none of them at the bottom of the window

A persistent Save bar implies unsaved state exists and clips on a short window. This direction states the same fact in two always-visible places: the title-bar chip (global) and the row echo (local — it tells you what saved). Failure is the only case that also raises a toast, because a failure must not be missed, and it never auto-dismisses.
