07 · Components, type, spacing — and the self-audit

Look at: the material ladder first — it is the whole direction in one row. Five elevations, and exactly two of them are translucent: chrome and floating things. Everything a user reads sits on the opaque plate. Then every control in its states, the six type roles, the spacing constants, and an honest per-page audit including the border count (this direction barely spends it, because depth comes from layering).

The material ladder — where translucency is allowed, and where it is not
1 · Window surface — opaque
--app-bg · the app's own floor

Never translucent. The window is a window.

2 · Content plate — opaque
tile · group · result card · prose

Every readable thing lands here. Depth = the shadow, not an edge.

3 · Chrome material — toolbars, rails, drawers
toolbar · settings rail · audio drawer

Content scrolls under it. macOS does exactly this, so we may too.

4 · HUD material — Quick Access, the ask bar
floats over another app's window

Denser than chrome, because what is behind it is not ours.

5 · Popover material — menus, toasts
NSMenu · popover · toast

Densest. It must be readable over anything, instantly.

✗ Never — prose on material
A paragraph of an answer, set over live wallpaper. The contrast depends on whatever happens to be behind it, which means it is not a contrast decision at all.

This is the one rule that keeps the direction honest. See page 04: every answer is on a plate.

Accent chrome = Ask. The capture outline is accent at 78%, glow at 12% — visible, not a highlighter. Attached · typing · sending · streaming · dismissed · detached (no frame) · excluded-app disclosure. Bar and panel are separate floating pieces, both material; the answer is on a plate. 4

05 Settings Sticky section title → group label → rows. The rail's selection is a full-row accent fill, never an underline. Rows ≥40px, groups 22px apart, one 12px inner padding on every group. Danger only on destructive or failed; the “Saved” echo is accent at chip weight, so it reads as confirmation rather than a new control. Idle · saving · saved (row + chip) · failed (chip + inline + toast) · shortcut recording · shortcut conflict. Rail is a floating material panel; the pane under it is opaque and scrolls. 2

06 Intelligence Model rows lead with the radio and the name; the verdict chip is the row's right-hand conclusion. Model rows 44px; the instrument gallery is a 2-col 20px grid. Four verdict colours are semantic: ok / tight / unusable / cloud. Cloud is info-blue, never green — it is a fact, not an endorsement. Verified · reachable · rejected key · installed · downloading · backlog running · cloud consent required. Same shell as 05; stock controls everywhere except the five instruments. 2

Border budget: 14 across six screens, against a ceiling of ~12 per window. Depth here is material, shadow and surface step; a hairline is only ever the edge of a piece of glass. Control rings (segmented outlines, switch tracks, chip outlines) are free by the standing rule and are not counted.
