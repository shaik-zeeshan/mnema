Direction 03 — Layered Glass

01 · Overview — the bento as a widget board

Look at: the tiles are opaque widget plates floating on the window — depth is layering (soft shadow + a subject-tinted glow from the top edge), never a border. The toolbar is the only material here, and the grid genuinely scrolls under it (see the 800 render). Four legal footprints only — 4×1, 2×2, 2×1, 1×1 — on one 16px gutter. The moments strip is a payload zone: the frames bleed off the tile edge and are clipped by its radius, which is what stops a bento from reading as a form.

1100 × 720 — default. Nine widgets, one --t-display (the 6:42 capture hero). The Ask row is a launcher into Quick Access — an answer never renders on this surface.
