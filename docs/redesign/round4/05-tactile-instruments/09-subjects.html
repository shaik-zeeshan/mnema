Page 09  Subjects — tiers by conviction, with the trace as the hero

Subjects is Overview's second destination. Everything here is a reading: a confidence is a measured quantity that rises with evidence and decays on a 30-day half-life, and the engine draws a hard 0.15 display floor under it. That is exactly the shape this direction reserves the recessed well for — so each subject wears a small strip-chart trace in a well with its floor line printed, and the detail page blows the same face up to hero size. Nothing on this page turns; the trace only reads. Tier thresholds, the floor, the half-life and the snapshot counts are all engine constants, not styling.

1 — the door on Overview

Overview's Subjects tile is already the door — two rows, each a subject, each with a chevron. It gains one thing: the count in the header becomes the link to the whole list, and the five conviction pips are replaced by the same trace the destination uses, so the tile and the page are the same instrument at two sizes. The toolbar back control is the one from page 08, unchanged.

What the trace states, and what it refuses to

It plots confidence, 0–1, one polyline per conclusion, the highest-confidence one in accent. The dashed red line is the engine's 0.15 display floor — a real constant, not a design flourish. The x-axis is snapshot time, so a six-week-old trace looks six weeks long.

It states no rate, no forecast and no "trending toward" arrow beyond the engine's own ±0.04 dead-band, because the snapshots are written on irregular events (a distillation that raised confidence, or a decay pass) and are capped at 64 per conclusion. A smooth curve there would be a lie about the sampling.

2 — the tier list, 1100 × 720

Tiers by conviction, strongest first. The three live tiers are the engine's own numbers — ≥ 0.68 strongly held, ≥ 0.30 forming (that is INITIAL_BASE, the confidence a belief is born at), the rest just taking shape — and below them the fading tier, which is everything under the 0.15 floor and is kept, never deleted. The axis switch offers the second cut: by movement rather than by conviction. Under five subjects the tiers collapse to one flat list, because sorting three things into four buckets is theatre.

Confidence is recency-weighted — a view warms with fresh evidence and cools on its own, halving every 30 days unless it is pinned. Faded views are kept for history, never deleted.

3 — one subject, open

The same face, at hero size, and then the story that produced it. The hero trace is the selected conclusion's own history — filled, because at this size the area carries the shape better than a hairline — with the floor still printed. Under it the other conclusions about this subject as a strip you can sort, and under that the trajectory: every confidence move, every piece of evidence that caused one, the take this belief replaced, and the moment it first formed. The two corrections that actually exist are on the hero: Pin (exempt from decay) and Dismiss. There is no edit — a belief is superseded, never rewritten.

4 — the states, and two things this page deliberately does not say
The Reasoning Engine is off. Subjects appear as the engine forms views about you, each with its own confidence trajectory. Turn it on to begin.
No subjects yet. As the engine forms views about you, each one appears here with its own confidence trajectory. Keep working and check back — they build up as evidence accumulates.
No subjects match “deepgram”. Search looks at subject names and the wording of each belief. Try a shorter term, or clear the search to browse all subjects.
Couldn't load Subjects. The one error state that replaces the list. A refresh that fails while rows are already on screen keeps the rows and says nothing.

Two refusals, on purpose. There is no "this is wrong, here is the right version" — the engine supersedes a belief with a new one and keeps the retired wording on the timeline, so an edit box would be a promise the store cannot keep. And there is no per-subject forget: dismissing is per-conclusion, so "forget this subject" would have to fan out into N dismissals and would still let the subject re-form on fresh evidence. Both stay off the page until the backend has the verb.

Format, unified

Today the index prints a raw 0.86 and every detail surface prints 86% for the same number. This direction prints 86% everywhere. Same value, one format — the decimal was the only place in the whole app that showed a 0–1 float to a person.

The x-axis is time here, and it was not

The trace is plotted against snapshotAtMs, which the store already writes on every snapshot. Today's sparkline spaces points evenly by index, so a six-week trace and a six-minute one look identical. Nothing new is needed on the backend for this — only the frontend reading a column it already receives.
