Honesty notes

Three things this page refuses to draw, and one it rewrites

Shipping todayThis pageWhy
A “Steering your dossier” rail — each authored statement shown as supports a conclusion Not drawn. There is no stored link between any standing statement and any conclusion. The rail picks the three highest-confidence conclusions and stages them next to your writing, which reads as causation the data cannot support. The honest version of “did my context matter?” is a real provenance field, not a layout.
A ✎ quill on the conclusion's subject name The quill marks your statements only. On a conclusion it implies you authored the subject. You did not — the engine named it.
A legend bar filled to 60% illustrating “confidence rises & fades” Dropped; the real conclusion's real bar does the job. The 60% is a hardcoded CSS width beside a caption about confidence. A fake number next to a real word is the exact failure G8 exists to prevent.
The wipe confirmation lists activities, conclusions and dismissals Also names standing context and ask history. Both are cleared by the same call and neither is mentioned today. A destructive confirmation that under-states what it destroys is a bug in the copy, not a wording preference.
Suggestion chips read as categories Labelled prefill:. They only seed the textarea. Topic is free text with no vocabulary, so nothing behind them is a taxonomy.
Backend asks

What this page wants that nothing serves today

WantedStatus
A provenance link from a standing statement to the conclusions it steered Nothing records it. Authored text is concatenated into the prompt block (newest first, capped at 2,000 characters, older statements silently dropped) and never attributed. Ask: record which authored statements were in the prompt for a distillation pass, so “this is what your context changed” can be true.
A signal when a standing statement was dropped for the character cap Silent today. Six short statements are nowhere near the cap, but there is no feedback at any length. Ask: surface the cap on the composer, the way every other budget in the app is surfaced.
“Mark wrong but keep” on a conclusion Does not exist and this page does not pretend otherwise. Dismiss deletes and vetoes; there is no edit, no soft correction, no free-text rebuttal. Whether that is the right model is a product question — it is not a mockup question.
