Anatomy

The four decisions inside “ask about this screen”

1 Two pieces, not one A persistent control pill and a dismissible bar/answer. A single fused panel means hiding the answer also hides the stop control — the one thing you need when it's answering.
2 Capture is implicit, the indicator is explicit No “attach screen?” dialog. The keystroke grabs the frame; a green outline on the display and a muted line in the bar say what was grabbed, and ⌘⌫ drops it.
3 A 42px thumb, not a hero The frame preview is a chip beside the label. A full-width thumbnail costs the bar's whole width to show the user something they are already looking at.
4 The frame is a source, not a mode The answer cites “this screen · 15:41:02” next to moments from history, so a screen-grounded answer and a history answer are the same kind of thing.
