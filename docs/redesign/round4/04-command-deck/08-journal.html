04 · Command Deck Page 08 — Journal (the day, and one receipt)

The day is a river. Every card keeps its receipt, and the receipt is a transport.

Look at: the river is a keyboard list — the same idiom as the settings rows on page 05, full-row accent selection, ↑↓ to move, ⏎ to open the receipt. The receipt is the one modal in the app, and because it is a transport it earns a real control row: ␣ plays, ←→ steps a frame, the scrub bar carries a tick for every cited frame. The deck re-labels itself — “Journal · Mon, Aug 3” going in, and while the receipt is open it swaps to the transport's own keys, which is how you know the modal owns the keyboard. Journal is not a third surface: it opens from Overview's Today tile (⌃D) and esc puts you back.
