Six inputs that answer “what does this cost me?”

Look at: every numeric control here renders its consequence in the unit you care about, with a denominator — a model is “1.6 GB · ≈ 1.4 GB RAM · tight on this Mac's 16 GB”, not a name; OCR pacing is a split bar whose two halves you can read at a glance plus one sentence of what it does to the fans; semantic search states its coverage against the frames you actually have. Everything else stays stock AppKit on purpose — six instruments, not a themepark.
