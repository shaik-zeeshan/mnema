06Intelligence — six custom inputs, not sixty

Same shell as page 05: one scroll, sticky headers, filter, status strip. The novelty budget is spent on six controls where the number alone is meaningless — a model row that renders a fit verdict against this machine, a duty-cycle bar that shows work against cooldown, a budget slider with a denominator, a ladder that marks your actual footprint on its own axis, a key field that states when it was last verified, and a toggle whose description changes to the present-tense consequence. Everything else stays a stock switch or popup on a 28px row.

1100 × 720 — Intelligence, scrolled past Providers to OCR. The section header stays pinned while its content scrolls under it. The OCR reading-pace control is focused; the inspector shows what that setting measured on this machine rather than repeating its label.

The six, at working size. Each one answers the question the raw value cannot: what does this cost me, on this machine, today. The rule they share — every number is rendered in the unit the user cares about, and every unit has a denominator.

1 · Rate slider with a consequence line
Frame rate2 fps
0.5124815

2 fps → ~1.4 GB/day, ~42 GB/month. Of the 512 GB on this disk, 90 days at this rate is ~126 GB.

2 · Retention ladder with your footprint on its axis
7 d30 d90 d1 yForever
you are here · 34.2 GB disk full

Keeping 90 days holds ~126 GB. Frames, audio and transcripts are deleted; context facts survive — retention never touches them.

3 · Duty cycle, both halves visible
work 20%cool 80%

Reads ~430 frames/hour; the backlog clears by 16:20. Measured here: +3 °C, fans off. It is a temperature control that happens to be a throughput control.

4 · Model row = size · quant · verdict
qwen2.5:14b Q4_K_M 9.0 GBfits on GPU
llama3.3:70b Q4_K_M 42 GBtoo large for 32 GB

The verdict is computed against this Mac's memory, not printed from the model card. The verdict is the output; the gigabytes are the evidence.

Where the restraint is. Everything else on this surface — Speakers, User Context, MCP, the rest of Ask AI — is a stock switch, popup or segmented control on a 28px row. Nineteen of the twenty-two Intelligence settings use no custom input at all.
