Why a bar, not the window

A 1120×720 palette covers the thing you are asking about. The bar is 560×76 and top-centre — the one region of a Mac screen that is almost always chrome.

Two objects, not one

Control pill and answer panel are detached. Hiding the answer leaves you the stop control; a fused bar would hide both.

The capture is implicit

No “attach screen?” dialog. The frame is taken on collapse and the indicator is explicit — outlined on screen, chipped in the sentence.

It is the same frame

What gets sent is the capture Mnema already took, at 14:36:02 — so the answer cites a moment that exists on your timeline afterwards.
