01Overview — the bento in pro-app chrome

The bento survives intact; what changes is the chrome around it. Look at three things: the tool strip under the title bar (dense, 22px controls, contextual to the surface), the right inspector carrying the detail of whatever tile row is selected — so a tile can stay a headline and never grow a second column — and the status strip welded to the window's bottom edge, which carries capture rate, disk cost today, and the write state. Tiles get flatter, denser chrome: a hairline under each tile header instead of a card look. Second frame is the 800×600 floor, where the inspector collapses and the strip degrades.

1100 × 720 — default. 4-column bento, inspector open on the Conversations tile's selected row. Bordered containers in this window: 6 — the window frame, three hairline strip separations, the inspector's left edge, and the selection ring. Tiles are fills, not boxes; control bezels are free. Ceiling is ~12.
