6 · Where this direction knowingly spends.

Density

22px tool-strip controls and 24px status strip are below the 28px row floor — but they are chrome, and the 28px hit target is preserved by their transparent padding. Content rows are never below 28.

The inspector's width

256px is real estate the other four directions spend on content. It buys the same detail surface on four different screens and stops every tile and cell from growing a second column.

Two permanent strips

54px of vertical chrome under the title bar. The trade is that capture rate, disk cost and save state are structurally unclippable — the founder's autosave complaint cannot recur.
